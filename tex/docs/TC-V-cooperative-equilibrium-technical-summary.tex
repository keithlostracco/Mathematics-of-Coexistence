\subsection{Model}\label{model}

Each agent selects from two meta-strategies per interaction:
\textbf{Cooperate} (respect boundary constraints, accept the constrained
allocation, incur coordination cost \(c > 0\)) or \textbf{Defect}
(violate boundary constraints, trigger conflict contest and/or
deception, incur associated thermodynamic costs). Payoffs are
denominated in conserved physical energy, importing three structural
constraints: conservation (conflict cannot create energy), positivity of
costs (all physical actions require energy), and persistent state
coupling (payoffs depend on the network friction state \(\phi\)).

For a pairwise interaction over resource \(E_R > 0\) with exploitation
efficiency \(\theta \in (0,1)\), friction multiplier
\(\Phi = 1 + \kappa(\psi^{\text{off}} + \psi^{\text{def}})\), and
coordination cost \(c \ll E_R\), the single-round payoffs are:

\[T = E_R\left[1 - \theta(1 + \kappa\psi^{\text{off}})\right], \qquad R = \frac{E_R}{2} - c\]

\[P = \frac{E_R}{2}\left(1 - \frac{\Phi}{2}\right), \qquad S = -\theta E_R\kappa\psi^{\text{def}}\]

When \(\Phi > 2\) (equivalently \(\kappa\psi > 1\), combined boundary
damage above \(1/\kappa\) --- the destructive regime): \(P < 0\), so
mutual defection is net-negative. The baseline parameterization
(\(E_R = 100\), \(c = 2\), \(\theta = 0.15\),
\(\psi^{\text{off}} = 0.15\), \(\psi^{\text{def}} = 0.25\),
\(\kappa = 2\)) gives \(\Phi = 1.8\), \(T = 80.5\), \(R = 48\),
\(P = 5\), \(S = -7.5\) --- the mild regime, with \(P > 0\); the
destructive parameterization (\(\psi^{\text{off}} = 0.25\),
\(\psi^{\text{def}} = 0.35\), so \(\psi = 0.6 > 1/2\)) gives
\(\Phi = 2.2\), \(T = 77.5\), \(P = -5\), \(S = -10.5\).

The discount factor \(\delta = \sigma \cdot e^{-r}\) captures how agents
value future payoffs (\(\sigma\): survival probability, \(r\):
time-preference rate). In the \(N\)-player public goods game, \(N\)
agents choose to cooperate (contribute \(c_i\)) or defect; contributions
multiply by \(\alpha > 1\) and are split equally.

\subsection{Results}\label{results}

\textbf{Proposition (Physical Prisoner's Dilemma).} Under the
energy-based payoff model with physical constraints (\(E_R > 0\),
\(c \ll E_R\)), the single-round game has the PD structure
\(T > R > P > S\) when
\(\theta < (1/2 + c/E_R)/(1 + \kappa\psi^{\text{off}})\) and
\(1 < \Phi < \Phi_{\text{PD}} = 2 + 4\theta\kappa\psi^{\text{def}}\).
Defection is the dominant strategy; the unique NE is \((D,D)\) with
payoffs \((P,P)\), even though \((C,C)\) is Pareto-superior.

\textbf{Theorem 11 (The Cooperation Theorem).} In the infinitely
repeated energy-based game with discount factor \(\delta\) and the PD
ordering (\(P > S\)), universal cooperation \((C,C)\) in every period is
sustainable as a Nash Equilibrium (under grim trigger) if and only if:

\[\delta \geq \delta^* = \frac{T - R}{T - P}\]

In the Chicken regime (\(S > P\)) the threshold is instead
\((T-R)/(T-S)\). Under the baseline physical parameterization:
\(\delta^* = \frac{80.5 - 48}{80.5 - 5} = \frac{32.5}{75.5} \approx 0.430\).

\textbf{Corollary 11.1 (Low Cooperation Threshold).} Under the physical
payoff model, \(\delta^* < 0.5\) if and only if \(T + P < 2R\); this
holds directly at baseline (\(T + P = 85.5 < 96 = 2R\)), giving
\(\delta^* \approx 0.430\) --- agents need only value the future at more
than approximately 43\% of the present to sustain cooperation. In the
destructive regime, a sufficient condition for \(\delta^* < 0.5\) is
that both \(\Phi > 2\) (net-negative mutual defection) and
\(c < (\theta E_R/2)(1 + \kappa\psi^{\text{off}})\) hold.

\textbf{Theorem 12 (Network-Adjusted Cooperation).} When network
friction effects are included, the effective punishment payoff becomes
\(\tilde{P} = P - C_{\text{defector}}^{\text{cascade}} / (1/\epsilon)\),
where
\(C_{\text{defector}}^{\text{cascade}} = \eta v M \bar{E} / (N\epsilon)\).
The network-adjusted threshold:

\[\tilde{\delta}^* = \frac{T - R}{T - \tilde{P}} < \delta^*\]

Network friction makes cooperation strictly easier to sustain. Under
baseline parameters: \(\tilde{\delta}^* = 0.185\).

\textbf{Theorem 13 (\(N\)-Player Cooperation).} In the infinitely
repeated \(N\)-player energy public goods game with grim trigger,
cooperation is a NE if:

\[\delta \geq \delta_N^* = \frac{c(N - \alpha)}{\alpha(N-1)c + N\phi_0\bar{E}}\]

For \(\phi_0 > 0\):
\(\lim_{N \to \infty} \delta_N^* = c / (\alpha c + \phi_0 \bar{E}) < 1\).
Cooperation remains accessible even in arbitrarily large populations.

\textbf{Corollary 13.1 (Cooperation Strengthens with Friction).}
\(\partial \delta_N^* / \partial \phi_0 < 0\). Higher network friction
makes the all-defect state harsher, easing the cooperation condition.

\textbf{Corollary 13.2 (Cooperation Strengthens with Scale).} When
\(\phi_0 > 0\), \(\delta_N^*\) remains bounded below 1 as
\(N \to \infty\), unlike the frictionless case, where the threshold
rises to the ceiling \(1/\alpha\) (approaching \(1\) as
\(\alpha \to 1^+\), the canonical free-rider collapse).

\textbf{Theorem 14 (Defection Invasion Barrier).} In a population of
\(N\) TFT-playing agents with \(\delta > \delta^*\), a mutant
Always-Defect agent earns
\(V_{\text{ALLD}} = T + \delta P / (1 - \delta)\), strictly less than
\(V_{\text{TFT}} = R / (1 - \delta)\). The ALLD mutant is dominated:
defection cannot invade a cooperative population.

\textbf{Theorem 15 (Defection Profit Erasure).} An agent that defects
once against a TFT population and returns to cooperation incurs a net
lifetime loss when \(\delta > (T-R)/(R-S)\). Under baseline parameters:
\(\delta > 0.586\) without friction; \(\delta \approx 0.183\) with
network friction costs included. The short-term defection profit is an
energetic ``payday loan'' with punishing interest.

\textbf{Theorem 16 (Cooperation Maximizes Welfare).} Among all strategy
profiles in the repeated \(N\)-player game, universal cooperation
uniquely maximizes per-period system welfare:

\[SW^{CC} = N(\alpha - 1)c \geq SW^s \quad \forall \text{ strategy profiles } s\]

with equality only when \(s\) is also universally cooperative. The
welfare gap is:

\[SW^{CC} - SW^s = n_D(\alpha - 1)c + n_D\, \phi(s) \bar{E} > 0\]

for any profile with \(n_D \geq 1\) defectors; the ambient per-period
transaction tax \(\phi(s) M \bar{E}\) enters \(SW\) through
\(\mathcal{C}_{\text{total}}\) and only widens this gap.

\textbf{Corollary 16.1 (Unique Efficient Nash Equilibrium).} In the
infinitely repeated energy-based game with \(\delta > \delta^*\):

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{(\alph{enumi})}
\item
  Universal cooperation is a Nash Equilibrium.
\item
  Universal cooperation uniquely maximizes system welfare.
\item
  Universal cooperation minimizes total interaction cost
  (\(\mathcal{C}_{\text{total}} = 0\)).
\end{enumerate}

The cooperative profile is the unique NE that is also Pareto-optimal and
welfare-maximizing.
